Vindolanda Mystery

An Educational/Adventure History Game

This project delivers a narrative-driven educational adventure game centered on the historical Vindolanda fort and its iconic Roman-era tablets. Designed to immerse players in 60 AD frontier life, the experience combines historical accuracy with interactive storytelling to teach about daily routines, social structures, and cultural dynamics within the fort. Developed using Construct 2's accessible 2D game framework (free version), it features:

  • Branching decision systems where player choices impact relationships and outcomes.
  • Contextual knowledge tests integrated into narrative progression.
  • Interactive exploration of reconstructed environments and artifacts.

The game transforms primary source materials into engaging educational tools, allowing players to investigate historical mysteries while developing critical thinking about Roman Britain.
