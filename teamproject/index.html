Team Project

El Bloob

This alpha version showcases our university team's custom-built C++ game engine powering a competitive paint-control arena. Supporting 4-player online matches on PC and 2-player local split-screen on PS4, players command spherical vehicles tasked with coating terrain, structures, and objects in their team's color. Strategic paint resource management is core: player balls deplete paint through movement, weapon firing (paint sprayers, pistols, area-coating rockets), and enemy hits. Teams replenish at dedicated paint pools while utilizing speed-boost and high-jump pickups scattered across maps. AI minions autonomously spread paint, capture points, and can be absorbed by players for instant paint replenishment, creating dynamic risk-reward scenarios.

My Role as Gameplay Programmer:

  • Owned core player systems: Ball physics, paint resource logic (depletion/refill), weapon interaction.
  • Designed & implemented the data-driven map architecture for dynamic object/mechanic placement.
  • Developed the real-time territory scoring system tracking environmental dominance.
